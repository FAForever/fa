- (#XXXX) Add an "ACU Transfer" lobby option

Adds a new global lobby option that controls whether players can transfer their ACU to an ally:

- No ACU Transfer (default): ACUs can never be transferred, matching previous behaviour.
- Transfer on Disconnect: a disconnecting player's whole army, including the ACU, is permanently transferred to their highest rated ally. The ACU cannot be transferred manually.
- Free ACU Transfer: the ACU can be manually given to an ally at any time, and is also transferred on disconnect. In Assassination a confirmation dialog is shown, because giving away your ACU defeats you.
